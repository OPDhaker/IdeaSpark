# IdeaSpark 3.0 Playbook

Ideas Ignited. Impact Created. IdeaSpark 3.0 is a two-day entrepreneurship
ideathon run by the Founders Club at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
Our motto is 決意し、火をつけ、導く。 ("Resolve, ignite, lead.")

- **Dates:** 5 and 6 October 2026
- **Venue:** Faraday Hall and Mini Hall 1
- **Format:** two-day ideathon. Day 1 is pitching, mentorship and evaluation for
  every team. Day 2 belongs to the Top 15
- **Team size:** 2 to 4 members, including the team leader
- **Who can join:** students from any department
- **Prize pool:** ₹15,000 plus certificates

> [!IMPORTANT]
> On-site registration and check-in close at **9:00 AM on 5 October**.
> [Register here](/register) once you have your team together, and reach Faraday
> Hall before the desk shuts.

### Registration and check-in

Teams arrive at Faraday Hall. Check-in covers team verification, team ID and
registration confirmation, track confirmation, participant instructions,
seating and table allocation, and the event essentials. Check-in closes at
9:00 AM.

### Inauguration ceremony

IdeaSpark 3.0 is formally inaugurated by **Mr. Pratyaksh Dwivedi**, Advisory
Head of the Founders Club. The ceremony covers the welcome address, the vision
and objectives of the event, the tracks, the two-day format, the judges and
mentors, and the rules and evaluation methodology.

The Founder Simulation kit is handed out to every team during the
inauguration, along with the briefing on its rules and its deadline.

### Filler game: Founder Simulation

The filler game runs in parallel with the main event and is due by the end of
Day 1. Each team gets a business-investigation kit of several interconnected
documents, and has to test its own analytical thinking, financial
understanding, decision making and ability to connect information.

The kit contains:

- Capital and funding information
- Network and stakeholder information
- Market and regulatory information
- Data and benchmark sheets
- Clearance and information sheet
- Decision and Deal Sheet

Rules:

1. No phones.
2. No laptops.
3. No photographs of the sheets.
4. The kit stays at the team's table.
5. Every answer must be derived from the documents provided.
6. Show your calculations wherever the sheet asks for them.
7. Submit only the completed Decision and Deal Sheet. The rest of the kit stays
   with the organizers.

The kit deliberately contains conflicting information and hidden connections,
so a team that reads a single document will get it wrong. Cross-reference the
sheets. Completed sheets are collected at the end of Day 1 and evaluated by the
organizing team.

### Round 1: Mentorship Arena

Three panels run at the same time, each with its own judge or mentor and its
own assigned teams. Teams move to the panel they have been assigned to.

**Pitch time is 7 minutes per team.** Cover the problem, the target users, the
proposed solution, what makes it innovative, the business or implementation
model, feasibility and the expected impact. Judges and mentors then interact
with the team and give feedback.

This round is not about elimination. It is pitch, expert feedback, a clear view
of the weak spots, and time to improve before Round 2.

### Round 2: Evaluation Arena

The same three panels continue after the break. Teams return to their own judge
for a focused evaluation against the official framework, the same four criteria
out of 50 that are spelled out under [Judging](#judging).

Judges also leave written remarks on the major strengths, the major weaknesses,
the potential of the idea, the team's performance and the areas that need work.
Those remarks matter when the Top 15 are picked.

### Closing and shortlisting

The organizing and judging teams consolidate pitch performance, mentorship
performance, evaluation scores, decision making and overall team performance,
and shortlist the **Top 15 teams** for Day 2. The shortlist is announced before
the end of Day 1.

Team verification, finalist registration, seating, final instructions and

### Triumph Talk XI

The flagship speaker session, and a conversation rather than a lecture. Expect
the founder's journey, startup failures and what they taught, building under
uncertainty, the realities of entrepreneurship, funding and growth, personal
stories, rapid-fire questions and audience interaction. The point is exposure
to real entrepreneurial experience, beyond textbooks and pitch decks.

### Grand Final

The Top 15 present their refined ideas to a final panel made up of the invited
speaker with one or two faculty members and judges. The panel scores the same
four criteria plus overall entrepreneurial potential, and weighs how the team
thinks and responds over how polished the deck looks.

## Judging

Every score at IdeaSpark 3.0 comes off one sheet, out of 50.

| Criterion             | Marks |
| --------------------- | ----- |
| Problem Understanding | 15    |
| Idea Feasibility      | 10    |
| Decision Making       | 15    |
| Coordination          | 10    |
| **Total**             | 50    |

What each criterion asks:

- **Problem Understanding.** Does the team genuinely understand the problem, its
  users, its causes and why it matters?
- **Idea Feasibility.** Can the solution realistically be built, implemented and
  scaled?
- **Decision Making.** Can the team justify its choices, handle questions and
  make logical decisions under pressure?
- **Coordination.** Does the team communicate and work together through the
  presentation and the Q&A?

A team is scored by the panel it is assigned to for that round, and its score
is that panel's average. Judges attach written remarks alongside the marks.

## FAQ

<details>
  <summary>Do I need to know how to code?</summary>

No. The four criteria are problem understanding, idea feasibility, decision
making and coordination. Nothing has to be built during the event.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I switch tracks after registering?</summary>

No. The track is chosen once, at registration, and stays fixed for both days.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Does every team member need an account?</summary>

No. Only the team leader signs in. Everyone else is added to the roster by the
leader from the [dashboard](/dashboard), and does not need a login.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>What happens if my team is not in the Top 15?</summary>

Only the shortlisted Top 15 teams take part in the competitive final round on
Day 2. Your Day 1 scores and the judges' remarks still stand, and the shortlist
is announced before Day 1 ends, so you know where you are the same evening.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I use my phone during the Founder Simulation?</summary>

No. No phones, no laptops and no photographs of the sheets. Every answer has to
come out of the kit on your table.

</details>
